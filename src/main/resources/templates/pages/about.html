Our Story

The Beginning

A Vision Born from Purpose

Vica Hydrate was founded with a singular vision: to provide India with access to pure, healthy, and responsibly-packaged alkaline water. Our founders witnessed a growing disconnect between the water people drank and the health they deserved.

Headquartered in Indore — India's cleanest city — Vica Hydrate operates under VICA TWAIN We set out to create a water brand that stood for more than just hydration. We built a brand that stands for trust, health, and environmental responsibility.

Today, Vica Hydrate is BIS-certified, ISO-compliant, and export-ready — serving health-conscious consumers and institutions across India and internationally.

Est. in Indore Madhya Pradesh, India
Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To deliver health, trust, and eco-conscious hydration to every household, institution, and market we serve. We are committed to producing water of uncompromising purity in packaging that respects the planet — making every sip a step towards better health and a better world.

Our Vision

To become India's most trusted and recognized premium alkaline water brand, known globally for quality, sustainability, and innovation. We envision a future where clean, alkaline water is not a luxury, but a standard for every Indian household.
