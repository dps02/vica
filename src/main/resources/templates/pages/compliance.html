Our Process

From Source to Shelf

Our 12-stage purification and quality control process ensures every bottle meets our strict standards.

01
Source Water

Raw water sourced and collected under controlled conditions

02
Pre-Filtration

Sand & activated carbon filtration removes sediment and odors

03
RO Purification

Reverse osmosis removes dissolved solids, bacteria, and viruses

04
Alkaline Ionization

Mineral enhancement raises pH to optimal 8.5 level

08
Seal & Dispatch

Tamper-proof sealed and quality-checked before dispatch

07
Labelling

FSSAI-compliant labels with all regulatory information

06
Bottling

Automated filling into sterile BPA-free PET bottles

05
UV Sterilization

UV light treatment eliminates remaining microorganisms
